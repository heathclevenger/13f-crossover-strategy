Overview

13F Crossover Strategy

The strategy evaluates individual stocks reported by institutional investment managers in quarterly 13F filings. For each quarter, it ranks the 25 stocks held by the greatest number of managers and the 25 stocks with the greatest aggregate reported value, then selects up to ten qualifying overlaps using their combined ranks. Each signal becomes investable 50 calendar days after quarter-end. New contributions and permitted sale proceeds are directed to the most underweight eligible positions so the active portfolio moves toward equal weight without adding to positions that are already above target.

Portfolio rules

Tax strategy

Quarterly contributions and permitted sale proceeds go to the most underweight current-list positions. Existing overweight positions receive no new cash until the other active names catch up.

366-day rebalance

Lots remain locked for 366 days. Once eligible, removed holdings are sold and current positions above their equal-weight target are trimmed, with proceeds redirected to underweight names.

Results use time-weighted percentage returns. Adjusted prices include splits and dividends; the benchmark is SPY total-return history.
